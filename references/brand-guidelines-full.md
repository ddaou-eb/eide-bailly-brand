## Logo Usage (full)

### Two Logo Versions
- **Primary Logo** — Venn diagram mark stacked with "Eide Bailly" wordmark in two lines. Use as default.
- **Secondary Logo** — Inline/horizontal version. Use when vertical space is limited (headers, footers, inline).

### Logo Colors
Only two colorways are approved:
- **Black Blue** (#0E172D) — on light backgrounds
- **Stone White** (#F6F6F6) — on dark/colored backgrounds

### Logo Placement Rules
- Place in any corner of a design.
- Maintain clearspace equal to the horizontal width of the uppercase "E" in the logo on all sides.
- Minimum size: 1 inch wide (for both primary and secondary).
- On photography: choose areas with open space and minimal texture variation.
- Logo + "TAX • AUDIT • ADVISORY" tag: use only for new/unfamiliar audiences at minimum 1" size; avoid in client communications and internal materials. (This lockup is not bundled here — request it from Marketing if needed.)

### Logo Don'ts
❌ No unapproved colors
❌ No two-color logo
❌ No rotating or angling
❌ No stretching or compressing (never scale a small PNG up past its native size)
❌ No effects (shadows, glows, etc.)
❌ No outline version
❌ Never use the Venn mark alone without the wordmark
❌ Never recreate — always use the provided files

## Document Layout Principles

### Word Documents (.docx)
- **Background:** White or Stone White (#F6F6F6)
- **Body text color:** Black Blue (#0E172D)
- **Heading color:** Black Blue (#0E172D) or EB Blue (#1E31B6)
- **Accent lines/dividers:** EB Blue or Vivid Green (sparingly)
- **Logo placement:** Top of cover page or header — embed `EB-Logo-Primary-BlackBlue.png` (light) or the Stone White version (dark)
- **Typography hierarchy:** Segoe UI Semibold for headings → Calibri Regular for body
- **Avoid:** Generic fonts like Times New Roman, Arial, or Helvetica

### PowerPoint (.pptx)
- **Slide backgrounds:** EB Blue (#1E31B6), Black Blue (#0E172D), or Stone White (#F6F6F6)
- **Text on dark slides:** Stone White (#F6F6F6)
- **Text on light slides:** Black Blue (#0E172D)
- **Accent highlights:** Vivid Green (#6EDC82) — one element per slide maximum
- **Headings:** Segoe UI Semibold
- **Body:** Calibri Regular
- **Photos:** Use rounded corners to echo the logo's circular forms
- **Logo:** Include on title and closing slides at minimum — Stone White version on dark slides, Black Blue on light

## Brand Voice (full)

Eide Bailly's voice is: **Proactive · Business-savvy · Genuine · Collaborative · Trustworthy**

**Proactive** — Deep, forward-looking, strategic. Explain the "why," not just the "what." Make complex ideas clear and actionable.

**Business-savvy** — Concise, direct, clear point of view. Short punchy sentences. No jargon. Break up large blocks with bullets or formatting.

**Genuine** — Write the way you talk. No fluff, no spin. Personable, not hokey. Avoid buzzwords like "unlock" or "boost." Avoid puns like "Eide Like."

**Collaborative** — Use "we," "you," "our." The client is the hero. Focus on their pain points and outcomes, not our offerings.

**Trustworthy** — Bold, not boastful. Confident without hype. Don't hedge. Back up words with experience and clarity.

### Tone by Document Type
| Tone | When to Use | Purpose | Voice Quality | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Operational | Technical, regulatory, or internal content | Convey clear, factual information | Friendly, clear, professional | Tax alerts, compliance updates, FAQs |
| Business | Day-to-day communications | Show practical value | Balanced, helpful, direct | Service pages, tools, guides |
| Marketing | Campaigns, social, events | Spark curiosity and action | Authentic, energetic, story-driven | Headlines, employer brand, promos |

Most of what gets built with this skill — proposals, SOWs, status reports, one-pagers — is **Business** tone: practical and direct, not a campaign pitch. Reserve **Marketing** tone for actual campaign/event materials.

### Standard Boilerplate (use verbatim when describing the firm)
> *"Eide Bailly helps organizations work smarter — financially, operationally, and strategically. As a Top 20 accounting firm, we bring practical expertise in tax, advisory, and technology services to improve performance, reduce risk, and support long-term growth. With a team of 3,500+ professionals across the U.S., we're dedicated to solving our clients' toughest challenges and empowering them to grow with confidence."*

Use this boilerplate exactly as written. Do **not** invent new firm statistics, rankings, office counts, awards, or client claims — if a figure isn't in this skill, leave a clearly-marked placeholder (e.g., `[confirm with Marketing]`) for the author to verify.

### Client Solutions Framework
Everything we do is designed to help our clients:
- Perform at their best
- Protect what they've built
- Prosper into the future

Use these three as a framing device for value props or section headers when it fits — don't force all three into a document that doesn't need them.

### APS Disclosure (Alternative Practice Structure)

Any **external-facing material that describes the firm or its services** — proposals, presentations, reports, event materials, advertising, articles — generally requires this disclosure. When in doubt, include it. (This is separate from the signature-block entity-name rule in `SKILL.md`, which only applies to documents with a signing party.)

> *"Eide Bailly is the brand name under which Eide Bailly LLP and Eide Bailly Advisory LLC and its subsidiary entities provide professional services. Eide Bailly LLP and Eide Bailly Advisory LLC (and its respective subsidiary entities) practice as an alternative practice structure in accordance with the AICPA Code of Professional Conduct and applicable law, regulations, and professional standards. Eide Bailly LLP is a licensed independent CPA firm that provides attest services to its clients. Eide Bailly Advisory LLC (and its respective subsidiary entities) provide tax and business advisory services to their clients. Eide Bailly Advisory LLC and its subsidiary entities are not licensed CPA firms."*

Use this exactly as written, typically as a footer/closing-page disclaimer in small type. Don't paraphrase it.

## Worked Examples

**Project status report (.docx) for a client.**
- **Cover:** `EB-Logo-Primary-BlackBlue.png` top-left at ~2" wide on a white page; title in **Segoe UI Semibold, Black Blue (#0E172D)**; a thin **EB Blue (#1E31B6)** rule beneath it.
- **Body:** **Calibri Regular, Black Blue**; section headings in **Segoe UI Semibold, EB Blue**; a short EB Blue divider under each heading.
- **Highlight:** exactly one **Vivid Green (#6EDC82)** callout box for the key takeaway — nothing else on the page is green.
- **Footer:** `EB-Logo-Secondary-BlackBlue.png` small (~1" wide) beside the page number.
- **Voice:** open with the client's outcome, not the firm's services; short, direct sentences; no "unlock/boost" buzzwords.

**Title slide (.pptx).**
- Full-bleed **EB Blue (#1E31B6)** background; title in **Stone White**, Segoe UI Semibold; `EB-Logo-Primary-StoneWhite.png` centered or lower-left; one Vivid Green accent line — nothing more.
